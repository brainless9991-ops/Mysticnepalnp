Package Overview

Discover the enchanting beauty of Santorini, Greece — where whitewashed villages cling to volcanic cliffs above the Aegean Sea. This carefully curated 7-day journey takes you through the island's most iconic landscapes, from the legendary sunset views of Oia to the black-sand beaches of Perissa.

Our expert local guides reveal hidden gems beyond the tourist trail, while premium transfers and hand-picked boutique hotels ensure every moment is comfortable, authentic, and unforgettable.

"A journey through Santorini is not merely a vacation — it is a passage into living myth, where every terrace, every caldera view, and every glass of local wine becomes a memory that stays with you forever."

Whether you're honeymooning, celebrating a milestone, or simply seeking the extraordinary, this package offers the perfect blend of cultural immersion, relaxation, and Greek island adventure.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 1

Arrival in Santorini — Welcome to Paradise

Fly into Santorini Airport (JTR). Private transfer to your hotel in Imerovigli. Evening orientation walk and welcome dinner at a caldera-view restaurant.

DAY 2

Oia & the Northern Villages

Morning visit to the iconic village of Oia. Explore the castle ruins and cobblestone alleyways. Afternoon at leisure before the world-famous Oia sunset.

DAY 3

Volcano & Hot Springs Cruise

Full-day sailing excursion to the Nea Kameni volcano. Hike the volcanic crater, swim in therapeutic hot springs, and snorkel at Thirassia island.

DAY 4

Fira & Ancient Akrotiri

Guided tour of the prehistoric settlement at Akrotiri — the "Minoan Pompeii." Afternoon cable car ride to Fira, the island's vibrant capital.

DAY 5

Beach Day — Perissa & Red Beach

Free day to explore Santorini's unique beaches. Visit the dramatic Red Beach carved into crimson cliffs, then relax on the volcanic black sands of Perissa.

DAY 6

Wine Tasting & Sunset Dinner Cruise

Morning tour of Santorini's acclaimed wineries, tasting Assyrtiko and Vinsanto wines. Evening romantic sunset cruise around the caldera with gourmet dinner.

DAY 7

Departure — Until We Meet Again

Leisurely morning breakfast with caldera views. Transfer to Santorini Airport for your return flight home, carrying memories to last a lifetime.

  • Hotel check-in is available from 3:00 PM on arrival day

  • Travel insurance is strongly recommended for all participants
